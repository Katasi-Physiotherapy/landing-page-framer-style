## Back Pain May Be Changing How Your Abdominal Muscles Work

### Why core control matters in back-pain recovery

Your abdominal muscles do much more than create a “six-pack.” Together with your back muscles, diaphragm and pelvic floor, they help support and control your spine and pelvis during sitting, walking, bending, lifting and exercise.

When you have back pain, your body may change the way your trunk muscles work. Some deep abdominal muscles may activate later or less effectively, while other muscles may become overly tense to protect the painful area. Over time, this can affect trunk strength, endurance, balance and movement confidence.

This **does not mean** that weak abdominal muscles are always the cause of back pain. It means that pain can change how your abdominal, back, hip and pelvic muscles coordinate during movement.

## Can core exercises help [back pain](https://katasiphysiotherapy.co.ke/blog/pinpoint-back-pain-muscle-pain/)?

They may help when they are selected for your specific condition and progressed at the right pace. Evidence-based physiotherapy guidelines support exercise approaches that may include trunk-muscle activation, strengthening, endurance, movement-control and mobility exercises for different types of low back pain.

However, **more sit-ups or harder exercises are not always the answer**. Some people need better coordination; others need mobility, gradual strengthening, improved breathing and pressure control, or support to return safely to everyday activities.

The goal is not simply to make your abdomen stronger. The goal is to help your entire trunk support movement efficiently, comfortably and confidently.

## When should you seek medical assessment?

Back pain needs prompt medical review if it follows major trauma or is accompanied by new bladder or bowel problems, numbness around the saddle area, progressive weakness or numbness in the legs, fever, unexplained weight loss or severe worsening pain. 

Physiotherapy should complement medical assessment when warning signs are present.

## Frequently asked question

### Will strengthening my core cure my back pain?

Not necessarily. Back pain has many possible contributing factors, and the right exercise depends on your symptoms, movement, strength and overall health. 

A physiotherapy assessment can help identify an appropriate and progressive programme.
